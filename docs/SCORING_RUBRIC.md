# Resume assessment rubric

The live analyzer applies the same five equally weighted categories to every original and revised resume. Each category is scored from 0 to 100 using only evidence in the supplied text. The overall score is the arithmetic mean of the five category scores, rounded to the nearest integer. Provider output is rejected if its stated overall score does not match that calculation.

## Score bands

- **90–100:** unusually strong, clear, and specific evidence for this category.
- **75–89:** clear and useful evidence with smaller gaps.
- **60–74:** mixed evidence; one or more meaningful improvements are available.
- **40–59:** substantial gaps or unclear evidence.
- **0–39:** evidence is largely absent or too unclear to assess.

## Categories

1. **Clarity and readability:** concise, understandable sentences and direct wording.
2. **Impact and achievements:** clear contribution, ownership, and outcomes supported by the text.
3. **Measurable results:** truthful quantities, scope, or results; no invented metrics are rewarded.
4. **Skills and experience presentation:** relevance and evidence connecting skills to work or projects.
5. **Formatting and organization:** recognizable sections, consistent ordering, and scanability of extracted text.

When a target role is supplied, the analyzer separately returns a 0–100 job-relevance assessment plus relevant, missing, and transferable skills. This is not included in the overall score. A missing skill is not a recommendation to claim it; users should add information only when it is true.

Suggestions may rephrase only passages present in the submitted resume. The system must not invent education, skills, jobs, dates, certifications, achievements, or numerical results. Scores are assessment aids only; they do not predict hiring success, employer decisions, or ATS performance.

The five in-app library entries are clearly fictional, locally stored demonstrations. Their example scores are precomputed against the same category definitions and average calculation; they are not regraded when opened and are not endorsements by real people or employers.
